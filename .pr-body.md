# feat: post-merge deploy+verify stage + per-repo deploy.auto policy

Adds a post-merge **deploy+verify** stage that drives a green PR through to deployment per a per-repo `.otta.yml` `deploy` policy, plus the `deploy` policy schema itself. Default `human-approve` (and an absent block) preserve today's stop-at-green-PR behavior exactly — existing repos are unaffected. Provider logic is pluggable and env-driven (Coolify adapter first; `none` is the generic path); no infra or creds are baked into the plugin.
